# Surface brief: OnliFin marketing site (landing)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: PF or small-business owner tired of spreadsheets; must act = choose plan, create account. Proof available: product capabilities and plan facts only (no clients, metrics, testimonials). Constraint: signup flow/API contract unchanged; future bank integrations stay "future".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one adding-machine tape. Control is shown as entries printed line by line, subtotalled per context (PF / PJ), forecast dashed, closing in one underlined total. Refuses the SaaS hero + mockup + card grid.
OWN-WORLD: Graphite desk ground; tape paper cold white with serrated edges, no radius, no shadow stack. Two ribbon inks only: near-black for entries, vermilion red for negatives/subtotals/the live line. Heavy condensed display (Big Shoulders Display) for headlines and giant figures; Martian Mono for tape data; Hanken Grotesk for reading text. Ruled rows, tabular numerals, double underline for totals. Raises: solid ink = realized, dashed = forecast (sewing-pattern grading); numbers settle character by character (split-flap discipline); red reserved for the line in motion (paper menagerie).
STORY: Visitor sees a messy pile become one balanced total; understands PF+PJ in one system with separate contexts; believes it because the mechanism (import, reconcile, forecast) is shown working on labeled sample data; acts via "Escolher plano" / "Começar grátis".
